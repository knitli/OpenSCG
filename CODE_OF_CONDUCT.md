# Code of conduct

OpenSCG brings together people from very different backgrounds: classification professionals, policy staff, developers, and members of the public. Treat everyone with respect.

## Expected

- Be patient with people who know a different part of the problem than you do.
- Criticize ideas, not people.
- Write plainly. Explain acronyms the first time you use them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Pressuring anyone to share information they are not allowed to share.
- **Posting real, controlled, or classified information.** This is removed immediately, and may be reported to the appropriate authority.

## Reporting

Email **hello@knit.li** with the subject line "OpenSCG conduct". Reports are handled privately by maintainers not involved in the incident. Maintainers may remove content or block participants who break this code.
